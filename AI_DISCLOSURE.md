# AI use disclosure

This work is done with **AI assistance** — primarily **Claude (Anthropic), via Claude Code** —
used as a pair-programmer and research assistant across code, experiments, analysis, and
writing.

What that means here, concretely:

- **The research is human-directed.** I choose the questions, the hypotheses, and what counts
  as a result. Every experiment is pre-registered by me *before* I see the outcome.

- **Every number is real and independently run.** No figure in this portfolio is
  AI-estimated or asserted without a script I executed and can reproduce. Negative and
  boundary results are reported the same way as the positive ones — that discipline is the
  point of the work, and it is not delegated.

- **I review and own every line.** AI-generated code is read, understood, and tested by me
  before it ships, and I can explain any of it. The same goes for prose, and for any
  upstream bug reports or pull requests.

- **Upstream contributions** to third-party projects disclose AI assistance per that
  project's policy (an `Assisted-by:` trailer, a PR-template checkbox, or a sentence in the
  PR body), and I only file a fix I have reproduced and can defend.

If you find something that looks machine-generated and wrong, that is on me — open an issue.
