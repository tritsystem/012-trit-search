# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Keep discussion technical and specific.
Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments are not welcome here and
will get you removed.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and to public spaces when you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

This is a single-maintainer project. Report problems to **gbranaa4@gmail.com**.
Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove comments,
commits, issues, and PRs that violate this, and may block accounts for
repeated or serious violations.
